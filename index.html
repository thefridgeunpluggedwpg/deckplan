Lot & house (site plan)

Setbacks on the site plan (a = sides, b = rear) are calculated from the lot, the house, and the deck's offset from the house's left corner. Leave a field blank only if you don't know it — it will be marked VERIFY.

Deck

The wing is framed as its own zone, with its own beams and footings. The joint between the zones gets a doubled, through-bolted rim (VERIFY with the City).

House wall: doors & windows

Manitoba ITS Guide 06 asks for the doors to the deck and any basement windows under or near it. Measure each one from the deck's left end.

Footings

Johnny's rule (stricter than the City guide, so it wins): any permit deck over 2'-0" above finished grade uses Gary Taylor helical piles, installed by a certified installer. Pylex piles only when the walking surface is 2'-0" or lower above finished grade (typically no height-based permit). Pile capacity and similar pile notes are listed as info, not as items to verify.

Ledger

The City deck guide doesn't set ledger fastening. The drawings mark it "verify". Confirm it with the plan examiner.
